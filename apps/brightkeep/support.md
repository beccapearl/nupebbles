## Getting started

Pick the rooms in your house, then add tasks to each one with a simple schedule: every day, certain weekdays, every 2 weeks, monthly, every 3 or 6 months, or yearly.

**Today** shows what's due, room by room.

## Missed a day?

That's fine. Anything you missed waits quietly in **Still to do** until it's done or its next date comes around. Nothing piles up.

## Quick clean and helpers

Pick the rooms, choose who's helping, and start the timer. Everyone checks off tasks on one shared list.

**Quick clean** and helpers are part of the one-time unlock.

## Reminders

Reminders are optional. You can have up to three a day, and they only arrive on days with something to do.

Reminders are part of the one-time unlock.

## The one-time unlock

One payment unlocks Quick clean, helpers, reminders, and backup & restore. There's no subscription.

If you reinstall BrightKeep or get a new iPhone, open **Settings** and tap **Restore purchase** while signed in to the same Apple Account.

## Your data

Everything stays on your phone. There's no account, and nothing is sent to us.
