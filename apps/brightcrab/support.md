## Setting up a move

BrightCrab asks a few questions about the move — such as whether you are renting or buying, and whether children, pets, or a home office are involved — then builds a checklist for that situation. You can add, edit, reorder, complete, or remove tasks at any time.

## Keeping the whole move together

BrightCrab includes more than a checklist. Use **Boxes** to number boxes, record contents and photos, and mark fragile, heavy, open-first, sealed, or unpacked items. Use **Budget** for estimated and actual costs. **Notes** and **Contacts** keep move details, movers, landlords, utilities, and other people in the same place.

## Saving a backup

Open the side menu and choose **Back up data**. BrightCrab creates a backup file and opens your device’s share sheet so you can save it somewhere you control.

To restore, choose **Restore from backup**, select a BrightCrab backup file, review its summary, and confirm. Restoring replaces the BrightCrab data currently on that device, so make a fresh backup first if you may need it.

## Moving to a new device

On the old device, save a backup somewhere the new device can reach. Install BrightCrab on the new device, then use **Restore from backup**.

The one-time purchase is restored separately. Open the side menu and choose **Restore Purchases** while signed in to the same marketplace account that bought the unlock.

## Exporting a move record

Choose **Export to PDF** to create a move manifest from the sections you select. The PDF is made on your device, then the system share sheet lets you print, save, or send it. BrightCrab never receives a copy.

## Local notifications

BrightCrab can schedule a local prompt after a move so you can decide whether to archive it. Notification handling stays on your device; NuPebbles does not run a push-notification server.

## Resetting the app

**Reset all data** removes every move and all related tasks, boxes, photos, budget items, notes, contacts, and settings stored by BrightCrab. This cannot be undone unless you previously saved a backup.
