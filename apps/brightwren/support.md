## Starting spelling practice

Choose a list, hear a word, and spell it with BrightWren’s letter keyboard. The correct spelling appears right away. After a miss, you can type the word again, write its sentence on paper and check it, or continue with spelling only.

A missed word is added to **Words to Meet Again** and stays there until it is spelled correctly. You can practice those words at any time.

## Profiles and custom lists

The free version includes one profile. The full version supports up to ten profiles on one device, with separate lists, practice, assessments, records, and settings for each person.

The full version also lets you make custom lists by typing words or choosing them from BrightWren’s built-in groups.

## Assessments and portfolio records

Open **Progress → Assessments** to choose an available list and record a formal spelling check. Assessment history stays with the active profile.

Open **Progress → Portfolio report** to preview a learning record and choose what to include. The report is created on your device. The free version adds a Sample watermark; the full version removes it.

## Keeping a copy before changing devices

In **Settings → Records**, use **Save a copy of the record** and keep the file somewhere the new device can reach. That record file covers every profile and can be read on a computer or restored into BrightWren. It preserves the dated learning record, not every in-progress lesson state.

## Improving the speaking voice

BrightWren uses voices installed on your device. In BrightWren, open **Settings → Voice** to choose and preview a voice. If you want another system voice, use the voice settings on your device to download one, then return to BrightWren. You can also choose **Slower** while practicing a word.

## Restoring the full version

Open **Settings → Full version → Restore purchases** while signed in to the marketplace account that bought the full version. Restoring the purchase does not restore learning records; use a saved BrightWren record copy for those.
