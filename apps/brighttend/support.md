## Adding an action

Go to **Settings → Your actions → Add an action**. Give it a name, choose the weekdays it applies to, pick a color, and — if you like — add a short *why* line to remind you what it's for.

## Recording a day

Each day, an action has three possible answers: **Did it**, **Some of it**, or **Not today**. Tap one to record it. Tap it again to clear it, or tap a different answer to change it.

Anything can be changed later, including past days — nothing is locked in.

In the morning check-in, **Record now instead** lets you answer straight away rather than waiting for the evening, and **Leave it for this evening** keeps the day open until then.

## Archiving versus deleting

**Archive** an action when you're done with it but want to keep what you recorded. Its history and its color stay.

**Delete** removes the action and everything recorded for it. There is no undo.

## Moving to a new phone

On the old phone, go to **Settings → Your data → Save a backup** and keep the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone.

On the new phone, tap **I have a backup** on the first screen, or use **Restore from a backup** in **Settings → Your data**. BrightTend checks that the file is a valid backup before it changes anything, so a wrong file can't damage what's already there.

## Reminders and text size

Reminders are off until you turn them on. The evening reminder arrives at your evening hour.

BrightTend offers five text sizes of its own, applied on top of your device's text size setting — so if the system size is already right for you, you can still make BrightTend a little larger or smaller.
