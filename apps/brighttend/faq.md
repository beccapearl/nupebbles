## Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign in to.

## Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything works offline. Only buying or restoring BrightTend Lifetime needs a connection to the App Store.

## Where is my writing stored?

On your device only. Nothing is sent anywhere. See the [privacy policy]({{ '/apps/brighttend/privacy/' | relative_url }}) for the full picture.

## Why are there no streaks or scores?

A streak turns one missed day into a reason to stop. A day you answered *Not today* is still a day you showed up and answered, and BrightTend treats it that way.

## What happens if I miss a day?

Nothing. The day stays blank, and the line in History breaks there. Past days can't be filled in later, so there's nothing to catch up on.

## Why does a line skip some days without breaking?

Those days were never part of that action — they weren't among the weekdays you chose for it. A break means a day you meant to record and didn't.

## How many actions should I have?

Three is usually enough.

## Can I print a month?

Yes. Choose **Save this month as PDF**, and the PDF goes to your device's share sheet, where you can print it or save it.

## How do I change an answer?

Tap the answer again to clear it, or tap a different one. You can change today's answers and note until midnight.

## How do I delete everything?

**Settings → Privacy → Delete everything.** This cannot be undone. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored, so save a backup first if you might want it back.

## Is there a subscription?

No.
