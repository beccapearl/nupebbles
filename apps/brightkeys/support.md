## Getting started

Start with **What can I afford?** to see an estimated home budget. Then tap **Add a home**, answer the questions about it, and see what it may cost each month.

## Reading a home's results

Each home gets a plain **Green**, **Yellow**, or **Red** read, with the reasons in plain words. These are estimates from the numbers you enter, not a loan offer, so check them with a lender before you decide.

## The four tabs

**Homes** holds your saved homes. **Learn** has short lessons and a glossary. **Payoff** shows how extra payments could change a loan. **More** has your settings, text size, appearance, and backup.

## The full app

**What can I afford?**, Learn, and More are free. One payment unlocks the full app: adding homes and their results, Compare, and the Payoff tab. There's no subscription.

If you reinstall BrightKeys or get a new iPhone, tap **Add a home**, then tap **Restore purchase** while signed in to the same Apple Account.

## Moving to a new phone

On your old phone, go to **More > Back up your data** and tap **Make a backup file**. Save it somewhere your new phone can reach. On the new phone, go to **More > Restore from a backup** and pick that file. Backup and restore are free.

## Your data

The app keeps everything on your phone. There's no account, and nothing is sent to us.
