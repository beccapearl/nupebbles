## Links you tap

Some rows in BrightKeys open an official source page, from a government agency, Fannie Mae, or Freddie Mac, and each row says where it goes. If you add a listing link to a home, "View listing" opens that page. Pages open only when you tap. Your phone sends the request to that site, as it does for any link you tap, and that site's own privacy policy applies. Nothing else from the app goes with it.

## A home's photo

You take it with your camera or choose it in your phone's own photo picker, which shares only the photo you pick. The app asks for the camera only when you tap "Take a photo." The photo is saved in the app's private storage on your device.

## Backup files

"Make a backup file" (More > Back up your data) puts everything you saved in the app, photos included, into one file. The file has no password, so anyone who has it can read it. Keep it somewhere you trust. "Restore from a backup" reads only the file you choose. It adds what this phone doesn't have yet and never removes anything already here.
